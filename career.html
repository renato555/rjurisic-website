    • 1000sdeveloper hours saved

      AI Knowledge Hub

      An AI assistant I built that answers questions about how our applications work, straight from their source code. Everyone from other engineers and product managers to solution and network engineers asks it instead of the developers, which has saved thousands of developer hours.

    • 42.5k→850CPU cores

      Fuzzy LLM cache

      A text-similarity cache in front of our LLM classifier that handles 700 million requests a day. Near-identical messages reuse an earlier answer, so classification runs at sub-millisecond latency. Together with the text-similarity work I started in 2022, the improvements cut the compute needed from 42,500 CPU cores to 850.

    • 45→90%traffic classified

      GenAI classifier for A2P traffic

      I championed a generative AI classifier for application-to-person messages. Doubling the share of traffic we can label made routing cheaper.

    • <10 msat the 99.9th percentile

      New regex engine for packet inspection

      I led the migration of the service that applies rules to every network packet. Latency improved tenfold and CPU use dropped to a third.

    • 100M+messages labelled

      LLM-powered sender branding

      This started as a hackathon prototype. It is now a distributed production system that labels hundreds of millions of messages at single-digit millisecond latency.

    • 10improvements proposed

      Fraud leakage review for a major operator

      I defined a taxonomy for the fraud that gets through, analysed the traffic with our data science team, and proposed ten product changes.

    • 180people in the department

      The go-to person for AI

      Engineers and non-engineers come to me about AI tooling and product integration. I brought data science onto the firewall products and taught a 35-person non-engineering team to use Claude Code.

    Infobip

    Student software engineer, part-time alongside my studies

    My first job, split across two projects.

    IoT: SIM card lifecycle

    I helped redesign and rewrite the service that manages the lifecycle of SIM cards, breaking a monolith into microservices. Built with Java, Spring Boot, Kafka and RabbitMQ.

    Anam Protect: SMS fraud

    Anam Protect is Infobip's SMS firewall. It looks at every message crossing a mobile network and blocks the fraudulent ones in real time, for 1.2 billion subscribers worldwide.       Bitwise text similarity

      A new text-similarity algorithm built on bitwise operations. It sped up the search for clusters of near-identical messages and saved about 1,000 CPU cores.
