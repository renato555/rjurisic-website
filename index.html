About me

I like hard problems where you can measure the result: making a system ten times faster, or making an LLM cheap enough to run on hundreds of millions of messages a day.

At the Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Computing (FER), I studied computer science: first software engineering and mathematics, then deep learning, computer vision and generative AI. I started at Infobip while still a student, spent a year teaching at FER, and came back to Infobip full-time when I finished my master's.

Croatian is my native language, and my English is at a full professional level.

There's nothing more enjoyable than standing in front of a whiteboard with smart people and brainstorming solutions to a problem.
